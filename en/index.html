What we do · Independent label

From the first listen
to the last row.

Since 2009 artists come first. An independent home following them through every stage: record, stage, story, community.

01 Distribution

We bring artists’ music to every store, physical and digital. Special packaging, signing events and targeted work with digital partners.

02 Management

We dive into artists’ stories and perception to build positioning and growth. Not targets: visions.

03 Label

We spot the new trends worth slow work, building a long-term vision from the ground up.

04 Promotion

We tell artists’ stories to press, radio, TV and web. We promote their visibility wherever it matters.

05 Booking

Our most ambitious bet: producing and delivering spectacular live shows, from clubs to arenas.

06 Merchandising

We design and produce custom merchandise, distribute it online and set up points of sale at live shows.
